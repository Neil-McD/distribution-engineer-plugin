# Distribution Engineer

Your AI marketing employee, inside your own AI. Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT or any AI that takes plugins works in your
Distribution Engineer workspace: it posts your videos and posts on every platform with a tracked link on each one,
rewrites your YouTube descriptions in a format that earns clicks, puts your funnel link on your videos and profiles,
builds your offer, form and booking hours, and shows you which post made the money.

## Install

- **Claude Code:** `/plugin install distribution-engineer --marketplace Neil-McD/distribution-engineer-plugin`
- **Codex:** `codex plugin marketplace add Neil-McD/distribution-engineer-plugin`, then pick it under `/plugins`
- **Claude and ChatGPT:** https://www.distribution.engineer/your-ai

## What it connects to

One remote MCP server, `https://www.distribution.engineer/api/mcp`. You sign in with your Distribution Engineer account (OAuth); the plugin holds no
keys or tokens, and nothing runs on your computer. Every change that goes outside Distribution Engineer (a post, a new
description, a link) first shows you exactly what will change, and runs only on your yes. You can remove the connection in
Distribution Engineer under Settings, then Connections, then Your AI.

## What's inside

- **Skills:** Distribution Engineer's guides: how to work with it, YouTube videos and descriptions, captions, and where
  tracked links go.
- **Commands:** ready workflows: rewrite YouTube descriptions, place your links, post a YouTube video, post this, and write
  captions.
