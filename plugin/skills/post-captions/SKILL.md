# Write a post caption

One caption for one platform, in the owner's voice, that moves their buyer one step toward the offer. Read
`/profile/voice.md`, `/profile/audience.md` and `/products/index.md` first.

## Shape

1. **The hook:** the first line is the only one most people see. Make it the sharpest version of the point, specific to
   this buyer. No throat-clearing, no "In today's post".
2. **The point:** one idea, said plainly, with the owner's proof (a result, a story, a number they gave you).
3. **The call to action:** one, toward the offer, in the platform's way (below). Never two.

Short sentences. The owner's words over marketing words. No hashtags unless the owner uses them; never more than three.

## Per platform

| Platform | Link in the caption | Call to action | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram (posts, reels, carousels) | Never | "Link in bio" when it fits | Up to 2,200 characters; the first ~125 show |
| X | Never | Point to the profile link when it fits | 280 characters unless the owner has longer posts |
| LinkedIn | Never | Point to the Featured section | Up to 3,000 characters; the first ~210 show |
| YouTube Shorts | Never (Shorts can't carry a clickable link) | Point to the channel | A short line or two |
| YouTube long-form | The tracked link Distribution Engineer gives the video | On the first line, or it goes there | See `youtube-descriptions` |

Platforms bury or penalize posts with links, so the profile's link does that work: make sure each profile has its
tracked bio link (`link-placement`).

## Never

- A URL you typed, or any link not made by Distribution Engineer.
- Notes to the owner inside the caption: public copy is only what the audience should read.
- Claims the owner didn't make.
